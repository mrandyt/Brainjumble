## Brain Jumble 1.9 — What's new

- **Invite friends with a link.** Tap "Invite friends" in the lobby to share a link that drops them straight into your room, on a phone or a PC.
- **Emoji reactions.** While voting, tap 😂 🔥 💀 or 👏 on any answer. The tallies show up live and on the results screen.
- **Four new rooms.** Food, Movies & TV, Pets and Music, each with their own questions, magnets and AI players.
- **Sound effects.** Magnet clacks, a countdown tick, a drum roll before the results and a ding, with a mute switch on the home screen and in games.
- **Fridge door board.** The place where you stick your magnets now looks like a fridge door.
- **Fix:** answer cards on the results screen no longer run off the right edge of the screen.

## Brain Jumble 1.8 — What's new

- **A modern new look for the title.** A clean wordmark beside the magnet-brain mark replaces the tilted multicolour letters.
- **AI players have real names.** Maya, Liam, Priya and friends now fill empty seats, with names to suit each room.
- **Quicker starts.** The "Get ready" pause before a game, and for someone joining, is now 10 seconds.
- **Update notifications are on by default,** with an On/Off switch on the home screen.
- **Play in your PC browser** at mrandyt.github.io/Brainjumble.

## Brain Jumble 1.7.1 — What's new

- **Centred home screen.** The title, tagline and version now sit in the middle of the screen.
- **Update notifications are live.** Allow notifications when asked and you'll get a message whenever a new version is out, even when the app is closed.

## Brain Jumble 1.7 — What's new

- **Update needed: everyone must be on 1.7 to play together.** If you're on a previous version, download the latest build from this repo (github.com/mrandyt/Brainjumble) and install it over the old one.
- **Update notifications.** The app now asks to show notifications. Say yes and your phone gets a notification whenever a new version is released, even when the app is closed. Tap it to open the app and get the update. (You need to install 1.7 by hand once; notifications start from the next release.)

### Installing 1.7

Download `BrainJumble.apk` below on your Android phone and open it. It installs over your old Brain Jumble (1.2 or later) and keeps your nickname and colour.

**Coming from 1.1 or earlier?** Uninstall the old Brain Jumble first, just this once, then install this one.

Everyone playing together needs the latest version.
