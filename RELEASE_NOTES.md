## Brain Jumble 1.3 — What's new

- **Update needed: everyone must be on 1.3 to play together.** Older versions can't join or run 1.3 games. If you're on a previous version, download the latest build from this repo (github.com/mrandyt/Brainjumble) and install it over the old one.
- **More joining words, commands and verbs.** Every round now deals a spread of connectives (*but*, *because*, *with*, *if*, *when*), imperatives (*stop*, *give*, *look*, *shut up*, *calm down*) and a lot more verbs, so you can build proper sentences.
- **15 second "get ready" pause.** Starting a game, joining a game and finishing a game each have a 15 second pause so every phone can catch up first. The host's Play again button unlocks 15 seconds after the winner is shown.
- **Voting fix.** Fixed the bug where, at the end of a round or game, you sometimes couldn't vote, couldn't be voted for, or got timed out. Brain Jumble now freezes no more if you're moving a magnet when time runs out, signs you back into the game if your connection blips, and gives slow connections a few seconds of slack.
- **No more shared wins.** If two players are tied at the top when someone reaches 10 points, the game now plays on with sudden-death rounds until exactly one player is ahead.
- **Fits any screen.** The layout follows your screen size on its own. Fold phones and tablets use the full screen (question and fridge beside the magnets, answers side by side), and folding, unfolding or turning your phone no longer restarts the app.

### Installing 1.3

Download `BrainJumble.apk` below on your Android phone and open it. It installs over your old Brain Jumble (1.2 or later) and keeps your nickname and colour.

**Coming from 1.1 or earlier?** Uninstall the old Brain Jumble first, just this once, then install this one.

Everyone playing together needs the latest version.
