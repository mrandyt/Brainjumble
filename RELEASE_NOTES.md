## Brain Jumble 1.7 — What's new

- **Update needed: everyone must be on 1.7 to play together.** If you're on a previous version, download the latest build from this repo (github.com/mrandyt/Brainjumble) and install it over the old one.
- **Update notifications.** The app now asks to show notifications. Say yes and your phone gets a notification whenever a new version is released, even when the app is closed. Tap it to open the app and get the update. (You need to install 1.7 by hand once; notifications start from the next release.)

### Installing 1.7

Download `BrainJumble.apk` below on your Android phone and open it. It installs over your old Brain Jumble (1.2 or later) and keeps your nickname and colour.

**Coming from 1.1 or earlier?** Uninstall the old Brain Jumble first, just this once, then install this one.

Everyone playing together needs the latest version.
