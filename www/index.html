How to play

  1. Everyone gets the same prompt and the same pile of word magnets.
  2. Tap magnets to build your answer. Suffix magnets like -ing stick to the word before them. Tap a gap to move your cursor, tap a placed word to take it back.
  3. Answers are shown anonymously. Vote for your favourite, but not your own.
  4. 10 points per vote, 20 bonus for the round's top answer, 5 for voting for it.

To play together, friends install the app, then type your room code or tap your game under Open games.
