How to play
  1. Pick a room. Everyone gets the same question and the same pile of word magnets.
  2. Tap magnets to stick them on your fridge. Little round ones like -ing stick to the word before. Drag a magnet to move it, double-tap it (or drag it off) to take it back. Tap a gap to choose where the next word goes.
  3. Answers are shown anonymously. Vote for your favourite, but not your own.
  4. 10 points per vote, 20 bonus for the round's top answer, 5 for voting for it.
